Q4 dominates: Q4 2025 had 24,814 DTC orders ($1.88M) — that's 83% of the year's DTC orders in one quarter. Q4 2024 had 15,349 orders ($1.32M). Non-Q4 quarters average 1,100–2,800 orders. This extreme holiday seasonality is the defining characteristic of the DTC channel.

DTC Customer Persona

"The Holiday Gift-Buyer & Puzzle Enthusiast"

Your DTC customer is a 35–54-year-old, likely married, with a household income of $75K–$150K+, living in US suburbs (California, Texas, Washington, New York). They have a bachelor's degree or higher and heavily over-index on Q4 purchases — buying puzzles as holiday gifts. They typically buy a single bundle or series pack at ~$75–$86 AOV, are drawn to the mystery/storytelling element, and are highly likely to leave a 5-star review. Nearly all are first-time buyers (96%+), with only 2–4% returning year over year.

Key Strategic Findings (Combined)

1. Two Distinct Customer Bases: KS backers are male-leaning, international, enthusiast-driven early adopters. DTC/Amazon buyers are female-leaning, US-centric, gift-driven holiday shoppers. Marketing should address both personas differently.
2. Extreme Q4 Seasonality (DTC): Q4 2025 = 24,814 orders (83% of the year). This is a holiday gift product. Non-Q4 quarters average ~1,500 orders. Inventory and marketing must be front-loaded for Q4.
3. KS → DTC Gap: Only 12.1% of 19,200 KS backers ever buy on Shopify. That's ~16,900 warm leads not converting. A targeted post-campaign email/SMS nurture could unlock significant DTC revenue.
4. Strong KS Repeat Rate: 17.2% of backers return for the next campaign — well above typical KS repeat rates. Series launches are your #1 re-engagement mechanism.
5. Missing Pieces = #1 Risk: Overwhelmingly the top review complaint. Highest-leverage quality investment you can make across both channels.
6. KS = International Gateway: 23% of KS backers are international (UK, AU, DE, FR, NL, SG) vs <5% DTC. Kickstarter is your best tool for building international demand before expanding DTC internationally.
7. Discount Creep: DTC discount usage grew 2% → 28%. THEODDCLUB alone = 7,756 uses. Monitor margin impact. Consider whether loyalty pricing is cannibalizing full-price sales.
8. Aging Customer Base: 55+ grew to 26% (Amazon). 25–34 declined to 11%. Product and marketing should reflect this older, more affluent audience.
9. KS AOV Climbs: KS AOV went from $66 (S1) → $99–$102 (S2/S4), driven by better tiering and add-ons. DTC AOV is stable at $72–$86. KS backers spend more per order.
10. 2026 Encouraging Signs: DTC return rate hit 10.4% (best ever). New products (Silver Lining, Time Keepers) are performing. The catalog is maturing beyond series bundles.
