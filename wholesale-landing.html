❓ FAQ

Questions?
We got you.

What's the minimum order? +
12 units for your first order. Mix and match any SKUs you want. We keep it low so you can test what works for your store.
What are the wholesale prices? +
You'll get full pricing details once approved. The short version: 50% off MSRP. You make great margins on every puzzle.
Do you offer consignment? +
Not at this time. All orders are wholesale (buy upfront). But with 50% margins and proven sell-through, the risk is low.
What's included with my order? +
Your puzzles plus a retail starter kit: POS display materials, product cards, high-res photos, and social media content you can use.
How long does shipping take? +
Orders ship within 5 business days from our US warehouse. Standard ground shipping is included on orders over $500.
Can I sell on Amazon or online? +
Wholesale accounts are for brick-and-mortar retail only. We handle our own online channels to protect everyone's margins.
What if they don't sell? +
They will. But if you're nervous — start with 12 units, test it out. Our reorder rate speaks for itself. We'll also help with merchandising tips.
Do you ship internationally? +
Currently US and Canada only for wholesale. International is coming — get on the list and we'll let you know.
