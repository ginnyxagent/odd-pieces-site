PM job in one sentence

Choose the right concepts, get them made fast, launch them cleanly, then kill, iterate, or scale based on signal.

Non-negotiable rule

Do not ask, “What should we make this week?”

Always build from: audience stage + angle + hook + format + CTA.

If one of those is missing, the concept is not ready.

Weekly sequence

What the PM does each day

This should feel like operations, not homework.

MonPlan

Build the weekly concept sheet

  • Choose 5 to 6 cold angles
  • Choose 2 to 3 warm angles
  • Choose 2 hot angles
  • Turn those into 50 concept rows

Output

  • 50 rows ready for briefing
  • Every row has stage, angle, hook, format, CTA
  • No vague placeholders like “make something around gifting”
TueBrief

Turn rows into assignments

  • Send static and carousel briefs to designer
  • Send UGC scripts to creators
  • Send talking points to founder
  • List every asset needed

Output

  • Designer queue
  • Creator queue
  • Founder recording list
  • Missing asset list
WedCollect

Pull assets and unblock production

  • Product shots
  • Box shots
  • Puzzle table footage
  • Reviews, comments, reactions
  • Founder clips

Output

  • No one is waiting on “can someone send me files?”
  • Everything needed for production is in one place
ThuQA

Check the work before launch

  • Is the hook strong in first 2 seconds?
  • Does it feel like Odd Pieces?
  • Is the CTA clear?
  • Is the format correct for Meta?

Output

  • Approved assets only
  • Bad work kicked back fast
  • No “good enough” corporate sludge
FriLaunch

Package and publish cleanly

  • Name assets properly
  • Tag every concept by stage, angle, hook, format
  • Launch in the right audience bucket
  • Log spend and results location

Output

  • Clean testing environment
  • Performance can be traced back to concept logic
  • Learning survives past one week
Important distinction

UGC and CGC are not the same

Use different briefs depending on whether you want creator-made ad content or customer-feeling content.

UGC

Creator ad content

Made by a hired creator on purpose for the brand. It can still feel natural, but it is more structured and more ad-ready.

  • Use when you want clearer message control
  • Use when testing specific hooks or angles
  • Usually cleaner and easier to edit
  • Feels like: “let me show you why this is cool”
CGC

Customer-feeling content

Feels like a normal person stumbled on the product and wanted to share it. Less polished, less structured, more discovery-driven.

  • Use when you want trust and relatability
  • Use for discovery, gifting, skeptical-friend energy
  • Usually looser and more casual
  • Feels like: “okay wait, I found this weird thing”

QA

Pre-launch checklist

If the PM does nothing else right, do this right.

Hook check

  • Is the first frame interesting?
  • Does the first line create curiosity?
  • Would this stop someone cold?

Brand check

  • Feels like Odd Pieces, not Walmart game aisle
  • Clever, direct, weird in a good way
  • Not corporate, not generic, not stiff

Conversion check

  • CTA is obvious
  • The difference is understandable fast
  • The concept actually has a job
Review loop

What to do after launch

This is where the machine gets smarter instead of just busier.

Do this

  • Kill concepts with no signal
  • Turn promising hooks into more formats
  • Turn promising angles into more audience stages
  • Rework near-winners instead of starting from zero
  • Write down what won and why

Do not do this

  • Keep weak ads alive because someone worked hard on them
  • Make next week’s concepts from vibes
  • Forget which hook actually drove the result
  • Confuse pretty creative with converting creative
  • Let naming or tracking get sloppy
