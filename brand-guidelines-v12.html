01 — Who Are We?

Founder Story

Terry and Ginny started Odd Pieces in 2020 after moving into a 400 square foot apartment together and getting locked down at home during Covid with not much to do besides Netflix. Like everyone else, we noticed puzzles were suddenly having a moment, so we gave them a try.

At first it was nice. Then it got boring fast. You stare at the finished picture on the box, then spend three hours rebuilding the exact thing you're already looking at. Where's the mystery in that? So we flipped it. We made puzzles where what's on the box isn't what you build, the clues are hidden in the pieces, and the ending is sealed in an envelope you don't open till you're done.

We put the idea on Kickstarter not really knowing what would happen, and it kind of took off. Four campaigns, $1.49M, 25,000+ backers, 280K+ customers, and now we're in Barnes & Noble and 600+ stores.
