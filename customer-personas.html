The Gifter / Receiver Split

The big strategic takeaway across all personas: your business has a gifter/receiver split problem. The person buying is often not the person puzzling. That's why repeat rates are low — the actual end user never created an account, never got on your email list, and doesn't know THEODDCLUB exists.
